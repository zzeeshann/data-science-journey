NOTES: Three-line fair-use academic research excerpt from a published article. The translation is by Yogev and Yona (Ben-Gurion University); their 2018 paper is itself a scholarly study of *visual poetry* in this tablet. This passage is the moment Baal first wishes to contact Mot, god of the underworld — directing his messengers Ugaru and Gapnu to descend to "the hills at world's end" where the underworld's threshold lies. Thematically threshold-loaded: this is a descent-to-death-realm passage. **Important methodological caveat for Session 8:** KTU 1.4 is the 4th tablet in the Dietrich/Loretz/Sanmartín catalogue of Ugaritic alphabetic texts (2013 ed.) — its "4" is a museum index, not a content position. It is *not* a 12-cluster member by the bundle's hypothesis. It is included as a thematically-threshold Ugaritic wider-sample passage. The bundle's hypothesis assumed catalogue numbers across texts were comparable; the Ugaritic numbering convention shows they are not. The chapter for Session 8 should surface this methodological question.
