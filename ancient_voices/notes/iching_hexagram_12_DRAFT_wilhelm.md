## Hexagram 12 — P'i / Standstill (Stagnation)

Upper trigram: Ch'ien (The Creative, Heaven). Lower trigram: K'un (The Receptive, Earth).

### The Judgement (Wilhelm-Baynes)

> Standstill. Evil people do not further The perseverance of the superior man. The great departs; the small approaches.

### The Image (Wilhelm-Baynes)

> Heaven and earth do not unite: The image of Standstill. Thus the superior man falls back upon his inner worth In order to escape the difficulties. He does not permit himself to be honored with revenue.

### The Lines (Wilhelm-Baynes)

- **Bottom line:** When the ribbon grass is pulled up, the sod comes with it. Each according to his kind. Perseverance brings good fortune and success.
- **2nd line:** They bear and endure; This means good fortune for inferior people. The standstill serves to help the great man attain success.
- **3rd line:** They bear shame.
- **4th line:** He who acts at the command of the highest Remains without blame. Those of like mind partake of the blessing.
- **5th line:** Standstill is giving way. Good fortune for the great man. "What if it should fail, what if it should fail?" In this way, he ties it to a cluster of mulberry shoots.
- **Top line:** The standstill comes to an end. First standstill, then good fortune.
